### Google Play — required before shipping A23
A23 adds `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE`. This is the
same permission family that got **AiZone** rejected on this developer account
("Play Console declaration and/or app description does not sufficiently
demonstrate the use of the FOREGROUND_SERVICE permission", version code 14).

Before submitting, fill in the Play Console foreground-service declaration and
mention the behaviour in the store listing, e.g.:

> The app keeps your live voice chat running while you use other apps, so you
> stay on the mic in a room after switching away. A permanent notification is
> shown for as long as the microphone is in use.

Without that declaration this build is a likely rejection.
