### New shared mechanism: per-product layout guides (`Item.meta`)
The client asked three times, in three places, for app-drawn content to stay inside a
decorated product's empty middle — chat bubbles (A3), the entry bar (A5), mic frames
(A19): *"انا محكوم بالفارغ اللي بداخله ... مليش علاقه بأي زخرفة"*.

Rather than three sets of magic numbers, one `Item.meta` JSON column carries
`insets` (the empty inner box) and `slice` (the 9-slice centre), both as FRACTIONS of
the artwork so they survive any upload resolution. The dashboard has a visual editor
for it; the app has one parser (`ProductLayout`) that every surface uses. Products with
no `meta` keep their previous look exactly.
